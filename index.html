About

I want to understand a system down to the mechanism that makes it move, and when the tools around me fall short, I build my own.

Working on development from first CAD to series production taught me that an innovation only counts once engineering, suppliers, purchasing and logistics can all deliver it.

At Mahindra I developed cooling and NVH components for electric vehicles and worked with suppliers in India, Europe and China. When finding where stock sat in SAP became a daily chore, I built a supply-chain visualizer to do it for me. Now in Germany, I'm writing my own reinforcement-learning code from scratch to teach robotic arms to move.

Projects

M.Sc. thesis · ongoing

Reinforcement-learning control of a robotic arm

“Study on AI robot training and control methods for multiple multi-axis systems”

I wrote a PPO pipeline from scratch in NumPy, with hand-derived backprop, GAE, the clipped surrogate objective and Adam, and used it to train a 4-joint arm in PyBullet to reach targets. After 4,000 training iterations it reaches held-out targets to within 11.8 cm.

Next: obstacle avoidance, and a policy that generalises across link lengths and joint counts using graph and attention architectures (building on NerveNet, SMP, Amorpheus and MetaMorph).

  • Python
  • NumPy
  • PyBullet
  • PPO
  • Robotics

A bug worth telling

The arm kept getting penalised for touching obstacles its own sensors said were far away. The observation measured clearance to the nearest joint, while the reward used the real link surfaces. An obstacle 5 cm from the middle of a link read as ~25.5 cm. Switching to point-to-segment distance made the two agree.
